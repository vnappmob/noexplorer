Getting started

NoExplorer is a two-pane file manager: your computer on the left, your storage on the right. Drag between the panes to upload or download.

1. Install

Download the installer for your system from the home page or the latest release.

System File Notes
macOS 11+ .dmg (Apple Silicon and Intel) Signed and notarized by Apple.
Windows 10/11 -setup.exe or .msi Not code-signed yet: if SmartScreen appears, click More info → Run anyway.
Linux .AppImage, .deb or .rpm Make the AppImage executable first: chmod +x NoExplorer_*.AppImage.

After that, NoExplorer updates itself: when a new version is out, click Install in the banner.

2. Add your storage

The first time you open the app, Settings → Accounts opens. Pick your service, fill in the form and click Save & use. Each service is explained in Connect your storage.

3. Move files

  • Upload: drag files or folders from the left pane to the right one.
  • Download: drag from the right pane to the left one.
  • Or select files and press F5.

Progress shows at the bottom of the window. More in Moving files.
