NoExplorer is a two-pane file manager: **your computer on the left, your storage on the right**. Drag between the panes to upload or download.

## 1. Install

Download the installer for your system from the [home page](/) or the [latest release](https://github.com/vnappmob/noexplorer/releases/latest).

| System | File | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| macOS 11+ | `.dmg` (Apple Silicon and Intel) | Signed and notarized by Apple. |
| Windows 10/11 | `-setup.exe` or `.msi` | Not code-signed: if SmartScreen appears, click **More info → Run anyway** ([why](/docs/code-signing/)). |
| Linux | `.AppImage`, `.deb` or `.rpm` | Make the AppImage executable first: `chmod +x NoExplorer_*.AppImage`. |

After that, NoExplorer updates itself: when a new version is out, click **Install** in the banner.

## 2. Add your storage

The first time you open the app, **Settings → Accounts** opens. Pick your service, fill in the form and click **Save & use**. Each service is explained in [Connect your storage](/docs/accounts/).

## 3. Move files

- **Upload:** drag files or folders from the left pane to the right one.
- **Download:** drag from the right pane to the left one.
- Or select files and press <kbd>F5</kbd>.

Progress shows at the bottom of the window. More in [Moving files](/docs/transfers/).
